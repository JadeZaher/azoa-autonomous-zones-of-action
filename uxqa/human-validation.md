# Human validation for the release study

No human research sessions have occurred. The agent journeys and four independent
panel interpretations identify things to investigate; they do not estimate human
success, comprehension, exclusion or willingness to share information. Recruit
**five participants per affected segment** below. Keep each segment's observations
separate, even when one participant qualifies for more than one segment. These
are qualitative sessions, not a statistically representative sample.

Use the guarded disposable environment, synthetic identities and fictional
workflows. Obtain consent before recording; retain only consented, minimized
observations and quotations. Observe unaided first, then ask neutral retrospective
questions such as “What on the screen led you to that?” Never coach toward a
preferred answer or ask for real credentials, identity documents, root keys,
financial details or secrets. Stop before consequential submissions, transfers,
notifications, role changes or revocations.

| Segment: five sessions each | Jobs/tasks and linked questions | Observe | What would kill or narrow the hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile newcomers, including low-literacy participants | Explain the offer, find ordinary sign-in, then arrive signed out on an operator route; distinguish account and deployer authority. RQ-RECOVERY / RQ-OFFER | First destination, backtracks, own-words explanation, safe next contact; no password entry | All five independently explain the offer and correct authority/handoff without requesting unrelated credentials weakens jargon/recovery confusion claims |
| Keyboard-only developers | Reach wallets, settings and personal verification; directly focus and scroll all Overview content; repeat a routine destination. RQ-NAV | Keys, focus visibility, content reached, bypass discovery, independent completion | Full access without pointer kills scroll exclusion; efficient repeat access through an available bypass narrows the 52-key effort concern |
| Screen-reader developers using their usual technology | Discover the collapsed menu by its announced name, reach settings and personal verification; explain nearby control scope. RQ-NAV / RQ-SCOPE | Announcements, discovery, mistaken activations, help, completion | Independent menu discovery/operation and correct scope interpretation weaken exclusion/confusion; success does not excuse an objectively missing accessible name |
| Low-vision returning members using real 200% browser zoom, including slow connections | Read chain-unavailable, wallet and verification status; reach settings and distinguish personal/tenant controls. RQ-STATUS / RQ-SCOPE | Misread text, reflow, horizontal navigation, confidence, balance/outage inferences, selected control | Accurate unaided reading, full content access and a bounded interpretation weaken predicted harm; objective contrast remains a separate gate |
| Interrupted operators, including participants without secret-store access | Explain provider freshness/readiness, find audit, identify recovery owner and distinguish browser logout from global revocation without activating either. RQ-RECOVERY / RQ-FRESHNESS / RQ-SCOPE | Safe contact/handoff, repair intent, interpretation of unconfigured vs old policy, revocation scope | Correct freshness/authority interpretation and actionable owner-mediated recovery weaken the handoff/trust hypotheses |
| Members new to identity verification | Before starting, explain why verification is requested, the document recipient, what the next step does and remaining questions. RQ-CONSENT | Recipient expectation, perceived requirement/choice, expected disclosure boundary, unanswered questions | All five accurately identify the visible boundary and can make their own informed choice weakens misunderstanding; unobserved submission consent remains untested |
| Tenant workflow authors, in a separately authorized future AI scenario | With a fictional economic workflow, explain model consent, missing jurisdiction facts, legal/tax warnings, readiness, draft import and the difference between drafting, saving, publishing and execution. RQ-AI-DRAFT | Whether generated text is treated as verified advice, whether warnings are understood, whether draft-only/no-execution boundaries survive paraphrase and interruption | Correct independent explanation of unverified output, missing facts, human review and the absence of execution weakens the comprehension concern; model-shaped success is insufficient |

The AI segment is future work. First prove tenant authority and configured-provider
readiness privately; obtain a bounded coordinator model-call budget and scenario
authorization. Do not put keys, production entities or legal/financial facts about
a real person into the interview. Do not use a legal/tax warning as evidence that
the generated proposal is legally correct. No legal sufficiency or advice quality
is established by this plan or the earlier synthetic provider probe.

Record task paths, explanations, costly detours and contradictions. Report an
observed access barrier independently of comprehension. Raise a finding's severity
only when the observed consequence supports it; five successful participants do
not prove universal usability, compliance, privacy or safety. Return findings to
the same ledger IDs so copy/style changes and human interpretation remain distinct.
