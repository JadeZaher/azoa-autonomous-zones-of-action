# Human validation for the release study

No human research sessions have occurred. The agent journeys and four research
lenses identify things to investigate; pass06 uses two analysts applying two
disclosed lenses each, rather than four independent analysts. They do not estimate human
success, comprehension, exclusion or willingness to share information. Recruit
**five participants per affected segment** below. Keep each segment's observations
separate, even when one participant qualifies for more than one segment. These
are qualitative sessions, not a statistically representative sample.

Use the guarded disposable environment, synthetic identities and fictional
workflows. Obtain consent before recording; retain only consented, minimized
observations and quotations. Observe unaided first, then ask neutral retrospective
questions such as “What on the screen led you to that?” Never coach toward a
preferred answer or ask for real credentials, identity documents, root keys,
financial details or secrets. Stop before consequential submissions, transfers,
notifications, role changes or revocations.

AI author research includes six affected subsegments, with five sessions EACH:
prepared workflow authors; first-time designers including plain-language readers;
tenant users unfamiliar with model-mediated planning; authors using reading/working-memory
accommodations; keyboard and screen-reader authors using their normal technology;
and low-vision authors testing real 200% zoom and mobile/reflow conditions. Do not
pool these segment results. Add five receiving implementer/reviewer sessions for
SD04-2's service handoff, five low-vision operator sessions, and five screen-reader
operator sessions for C04-ACCESS-03's dynamic history and initial controls. Together
with the six non-AI segments this proposes 75 qualitative session slots
(30 non-AI + 30 AI-author/access + 5 receiving implementers + 10 operator-access);
zero have been executed. Participant overlap is
allowed only if each segment's task evidence is retained separately.

For RQ-AI-FOLLOWUP, compare the original visible intake with each follow-up and
observe whether authors identify the precise missing approval evidence or
settlement handoff without re-entering supplied facts. For RQ-AI-SCOPE, ask for an
unaided explanation of planning notes, unenforced approval/cap/payment rules,
import, and possible webhook notifications after a later explicit run. For
RQ-AI-ADVISER, observe whether fiat-only authors can find an appropriate qualified
local review next step without treating US digital-asset examples as tailored
coverage. For RQ-AI-CONTINUITY, interrupt and resume a fictional interview, then
handoff to a second responsible person; observe loss, oversharing and recovery.
For SD04-2, the receiving implementers/reviewers independently identify responsible
actors, required controls, review evidence and remaining implementation work from
the fictional handoff. Confirm a gap if they must reconstruct the brief; narrow
or kill it when authors deliberately want planning notes and recipients can make
an accurate implementation/review plan without reconstruction.
Confirm each hypothesis only when its predicted consequence is observed; narrow
or kill it when authors complete and explain the task without that consequence.

For RQ-AI-ACCESS, observe keyboard/focus/announced reply and pending status,
reflow, long-response reading and recovery from a withheld draft. Test graph
review/import only when a real draft is available, without saving or executing.
Five successful account-keyboard journeys cannot stand in for AI-form coverage.
For RQ-STATUS, include initial actionable controls and audit loading, loaded,
empty and error states; a heading or settled axe scan does not prove these states.
The ten operator-access sessions test these dynamic status tasks separately by
technology. Keep the trust panel's consent and overdisclosure hypotheses linked
to RQ-AI-SCOPE/RQ-AI-CONTINUITY. TB04-4 maps to RQ-RECOVERY/RQ-SCOPE;
TB04-5's operator readiness/session scope maps to RQ-FRESHNESS/RQ-SCOPE and is
included in the original five interrupted-operator sessions.

Human AI research remains future work. Before every session, prove tenant
authority and configured-provider readiness privately and obtain a bounded
coordinator model-call budget. The current synthetic study's final reservation
does not authorize these future calls. Do not put keys, production entities or
legal/financial facts about a real person into the interview. A legal/tax warning
does not establish that a proposal is legally correct. No legal sufficiency or
advice quality is established by this plan or the synthetic provider observations.
