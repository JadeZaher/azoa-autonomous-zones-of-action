## Human validation and refutation

No human sessions have occurred. Propose five sessions **per segment** below, with synthetic briefs and disposable accounts. Observe unaided first; ask participants to explain their decision and point to the cue that informed it. Do not teach the desired interpretation before measuring it. Use recorded screens for initial comprehension; any future live continuation requires a separately authorized provider budget. Stop before real disclosure, notification, identity submission, policy change, revocation or value movement.

Five qualitative sessions reveal mechanisms and individual paths; they do not estimate a population failure rate. Confirmations should include the participant's observed decision and the screen cue behind it. Refutation means the proposed mechanism weakens under these tasks, not that every audience or state is cleared. Accessibility or low-literacy differences should be reported separately if observed.
