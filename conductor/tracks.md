> `pending` (specified, not started) - `in_progress` (underway) - `deferred`
> (designed, activation-gated) - `shipped`. A track's `metadata.json` `status`
> is the source of truth, and the section a row sits in below MUST match it.
> Before this sweep five spellings were in use across eleven tracks
> (`pending`, `in_progress`, `in-progress`, `in-flight`, `active`), and four
> rows sat in a section that contradicted their own metadata. Known remaining
> outlier: `terminology-neutralization-cutover_20260905` still reads
> `in-flight`; that track's directory is owned by the live cutover and was left
> untouched deliberately - normalize it at that track's closeout.
